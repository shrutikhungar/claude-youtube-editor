🧘 Breathe This Daily to Quiet Your Mind, Open Your Heart & Bring Clarity

Sometimes the answers you're searching for don't come from thinking harder—they arise when the mind becomes quiet, the nervous system settles, and the heart opens through the power of breath.

This complete 30-minute guided beginner breathwork routine features 5 classical pranayam techniques designed to help you release overthinking, dissolve stress, open your heart, and reconnect with the still awareness already within you.

Whether you're starting your morning with renewed energy, taking a midday reset, or winding down your evening, return to this practice whenever you need a moment of stillness, emotional balance, and inner clarity.

✨ In this breathwork session you'll experience:
• 5 classical breathing techniques (Bhastrika, Kapalbhati, Anulom Vilom, Bahya Kumbhaka, Bhramari)
• On-screen visual breathing ring guiding every inhale, exhale & hold
• Deep relaxation, nervous system calm & inner stillness
• Gentle release from mental clutter and overthinking
• Greater emotional clarity, presence, and vitality
• Structured beginner pace with built-in recovery rest intervals

⚠️ BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Practise on an empty stomach. Skip forceful techniques (Bellows and Skull Shining) if you are pregnant, or have high blood pressure, heart conditions, epilepsy, glaucoma, hernia, or recent abdominal surgery. Never strain — if you feel dizzy or breathless, stop and return to natural breathing. General wellness content, not medical advice.
